# Swift Ship Tracker — Build Parcel Booking Flows

**Implementation guide · Step 04 of 10**

## Objective

Open the booking Screen Flow. Use Get Records to find existing Sender and Receiver records, or Create Records when new records are needed. Add validation for missing or invalid details. Use Create Records to create the Parcel record and populate its relationships and fields. Generate or assign a unique tracking number and prevent duplicates. Show a success screen with the tracking number. Save, activate and test valid and invalid submissions.

## Expected result

A successful booking creates the expected records and displays a unique tracking number.
